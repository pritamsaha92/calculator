Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Your privacy is absolute. This application operates entirely within your web browser. No metrics, tracking parameters, or analytical histories are collected or transmitted to external servers.

1. Client-Side Processing Paradigm

Our core assessment calculators operate completely via standard browser script execution environments. All data entry variants—including numerical measurements, biographical dates, and calculated configurations:

  • Are computed locally on your device hardware in real-time.
  • Are never transferred, broadcasted, or exposed to remote network APIs or server targets.
  • Exist transiently inside device memory spaces.

2. Storage and Data Session Lifecycles

This utility relies solely on browser session environments to improve performance and preserve values during local calculations. Closing your active browser tab, executing programmatic clearing actions, or hard-refreshing your window context instantly wipes out all running data points.

3. Third-Party Libraries and Trackers

We restrict remote dependencies strictly to official visualization tools (such as unified Chart.js delivery vectors). This system does not house background analytical tracking pixels, cross-origin marketing pixels, or performance telemetry mechanisms designed to harvest user data profiles.

4. Revisions and Modifications

As this engine undergoes structural compliance improvements to mirror standard medical and mathematical update logs, local functional properties might evolve. However, our zero-collection infrastructure principle remains continuous.
